The three leads

Two ways to get it wrong

The array goes LEDs up. The face with the XMOS chip, the 2 × 12 header field and the white JST connector is the bottom. Upside down, the lid stands proud on the JST and the microphones aim into the Pi.

Use the blue USB 3.0 ports for the array lead. They have 23.1 mm to the wall. The outer pair has 20.5, which a plug will just about make and nothing more — and both are measured to a wall that is now a doorway anyway, so a plug that overshoots simply pokes out of it.
